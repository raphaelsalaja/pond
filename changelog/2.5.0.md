## New

- Open Download Video or Audio from the File menu, the tray, or the New menu, and paste a link.
- Preview a video before you download it, then trim it with in and out points or the keyboard.
- See the most replayed moments of a video on its timeline.
- Export a link as video, audio, a GIF up to 30 seconds, or a transcript, to your library, a folder, or the clipboard.
- Pick which entries of a playlist to download.

## Improved

- Saves that come with site captions use them as the transcript, so Pond skips transcribing them on your Mac.
- When a site needs you to sign in, Pond offers to connect that source and tries the download again.

## Fixed

- The browser extension connects to Pond on Intel Macs.
- The browser extension connects to Pond in Helium on macOS and Linux.
- Switching or creating a Library reopens Pond when you run it as an AppImage on Linux.
