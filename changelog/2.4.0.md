## New

- Connect mymind with a read-only key, and the items you save there import into Pond as they arrive.
- Bring in older mymind items with Import mymind Export.

## Improved

- GitHub and Twitter Accounts pick up your browser sign-in from the extension, and saves that were waiting on sign-in retry on their own.
- Large downloads keep going as long as data arrives, instead of stopping after 30 seconds.
- Moving or trashing the save open in the full preview moves on to the next save, and the preview closes only when the collection is empty.

## Fixed

- Enter and Escape work in text fields again, so pressing Enter saves a folder name.
- Right-clicking a folder opens its menu without opening the folder.
- A folder no longer stays highlighted after you open a built-in collection like All or Trash.
- A split post isn't downloaded again after you empty the Trash, as long as one of its slides remains.
- Retry resumes a save that was waiting on sign-in, along with its remaining downloads.
