## New

- Video cards hover-play their video even when a custom thumbnail, generated thumbnail, or post screenshot is the card image, which stays in place when the pointer leaves.

## Improved

- Opening a video shows its colors while it loads, and the first frame fades in over them instead of appearing in an empty white box.
- Instagram videos use the creator's chosen cover as their poster instead of the video's first frame.
- Drag-select starts from any empty space in the grid, including the strip beside the first column and the space below the last row, and a click there clears the selection.
- Drag-select keeps scrolling at full speed at the window edge when smooth scrolling is on.
