## Improved

- Opening your library, switching libraries, and scrolling the grid no longer wait on reading library files.

## Fixed

- Syncing YouTube no longer signs you out of YouTube in your browser, and browsing YouTube no longer signs out Pond.
- A signed-out YouTube Account asks you to sign in again instead of syncing an empty Watch Later list.
- The Instagram collections picker lists your collections again after Instagram retired the list Pond used.
- Instagram Accounts on French and other non-English pages show your username instead of @?.
- Remove a failed library transfer from Library Transfers in Downloads with Dismiss.
- Moving the library folder while Pond is open no longer fails every enrichment pass, and Processing names the saves whose files are missing.
- On Linux, the AppImage reopens after you switch libraries instead of failing to relaunch.
