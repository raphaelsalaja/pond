## New

- The Inbox collects new Saves for review, and you can work through it as a full-page deck with the arrow keys.
- Drag Saves onto the drag to file ring to file them, choose its folders and actions, and open it over any app with a global shortcut.
- Files dropped from other apps go through the drag to file ring too.
- Pond finds Duplicates by their files, video frames, and audio, and Duplicate Review explains each group.
- Group Saves turns selected images and videos into one Save, and Split separates them again.
- LinkedIn saved posts sync as a Source.
- Modifier-click a Save to peek at it in Quick Preview, then click an image to zoom in.
- Triage the open Save from the keyboard, and undo or redo folder changes from its toast.
- Select Saves with selection circles and act on them from the selection toolbar.
- Capture from anywhere with the global Quick Capture shortcut, or from Finder with the Save to Pond Quick Action.
- Storage settings show what's using space, let you review cleanup first, and compress videos as HEVC.
- Library Health settings find problems in your Library and help you fix them.
- Start a folder from a folder template, give it a custom color, and search icons with your recent picks first.
- Create a Canvas from a folder.
- Export Files packages Saves into a named folder or a ZIP.
- The MCP server shows previews and prompts, and installs into Claude Desktop.
- Split Posts lists every post you split, and splitting can skip posts below a minimum number of images or videos.
- Pinned Saves get their own section at the top of a folder.
- Library Transfers show as rows in Downloads.
- Saved searches get the query as their title and a search card cover.
- The Recently Added menu offers date shortcuts.
- View a Save's thumbnail at full size.
- Empty folders offer Add Files, and import toasts offer Show in Library.
- Right-click empty sidebar space to open the sidebar menu.
- The app icon follows your macOS appearance settings, and sidebar drags play trackpad haptics.

## Improved

- Every icon is redrawn as a round-cap line drawing with one stroke weight.
- Each Library view has its own address, so Back, Forward, and the mouse side buttons follow your real history.
- Pages introduce themselves the first time you open them instead of opening a dialog.
- The Save details pane shows its title, note, and properties in one column.
- The update dialog and What's New show what changed with a picture.
- Search Pond in the toolbar searches the view you're in by default.
- Pasted and dropped items land in the folder you're viewing.
- Quick Preview springs open and closed, and the viewer holds steady while you step between Saves.
- Large folders and Settings open without a delay.
- The packaged app is smaller.
- Dragging Saves into folders is reliable.

## Fixed

- Dragging a Save no longer crashes Pond on macOS.
- Backspace restores a selected Save from Trash.
- Arrow keys keep stepping Saves after you click Favorite, a star, or a viewer tool.
- Enter no longer submits while Japanese, Chinese, or Korean input is composing.
- Keyword search stays inside the folder or Trash you're viewing.
- Eagle preview sidecars no longer import as duplicate Saves.
- Importing a file that's in Trash offers to restore its Save.
- Pasting an unsupported link again reuses its Save.
- Media behind a site challenge downloads through your harvest session.
- An empty scrape retries instead of marking the page deleted.
- YouTube Shorts harvest through their watch page.
- A disconnected Account with no stored handle reconnects.
- Downloaded media records its full size.
- Links in posts synced from X stay in the post.
- Native tweet cards render again, and cards made before 2.6.0 are redone.
- Live X screenshots wait for the post's media.
- Are.na full fetches page through every block.
- Watch checks skip items you emptied from Trash.
- Combine Images honors Post Cover.
- Notes keep their leading and trailing spaces.
- The browser tab title stays when the harvested title is part of it.
- The card cover no longer flashes while you step through the viewer.
- Cmd+A selects page text only inside a text field.
