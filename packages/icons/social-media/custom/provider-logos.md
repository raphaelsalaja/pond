# Provider logos

Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Ollama, and LM Studio SVG paths are vendored from @lobehub/icons-static-svg 1.95.0 (https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons), under the accompanying MIT license. Google uses google-color.svg; other logos use their monochrome SVG. Paths and brand colors are unchanged; JSX attributes and accessibility decoration were adapted for labeled UI controls.

Tumblr, Behance, Vimeo, Dribbble, Unsplash, and Bluesky marks are vendored from Simple Icons (CC0, https://simpleicons.org) unless noted.

Savee has no Simple Icons entry. Its tile uses the "S" glyph from the wordmark on https://savee.com, white on black like its app icon.
