# Roadmap

What's planned for Pond. Items have no dates, and the order isn't a commitment. To suggest something, [open an issue](https://github.com/raphaelsalaja/pond/issues).

- **Windows on Arm and Linux.** Pond runs on macOS 13 or later and on Windows 10 or 11 (x64) today. Builds for Windows on Arm and Linux come next.
- **Safari.** The extension works in Chrome, Arc, Brave, Edge, Firefox, and Zen. A Safari version follows.
- **More sources.** Sync saves from more of the places you save, alongside the [current sources](docs/supported-sources.md).
- **iPhone and iPad.** Browse your library and save to it from your phone or tablet.
- **Import from more apps.** Eagle libraries import today with their folders. Bookmark managers such as Raindrop.io come next.
- **Region notes.** Pin a note to part of an image or a moment in a video.
- **Plugins.** Extend Pond with JavaScript plugins that run on your computer.
