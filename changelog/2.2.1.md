## New

- Turn off Archive Web Pages Automatically in Storage to keep only the title, description, and preview of a web save.

## Fixed

- Trashing a download or clearing unfinished downloads stops its import, and Undo picks it back up.
- Tweet saves no longer fail when the author's profile picture can't be downloaded.
- The Video not downloaded notice sits above the zoom controls instead of under them.
